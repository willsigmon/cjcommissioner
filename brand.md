# CJ Turrentine Campaign Brand

## Positioning

**Proof in the Work** is the campaign's organizing idea. The site should make a
simple case quickly: CJ Turrentine has served, has measurable community results,
and is ready to bring that same service-first approach to Vance County
Commission District 3.

## Audience

- District 3 voters who need a quick, trustworthy introduction to CJ
- Community members who know the work but not the full story
- Volunteers and donors looking for a clear next action
- Local press and civic partners checking claims and campaign status

## Voice

- First person when CJ tells his story; direct third person for campaign facts
- Specific and grounded: outcomes before titles or affiliations
- Neighbor-to-neighbor, never consultant jargon
- Confident without exaggeration
- Service over politics

## Core messages

1. **A public servant. A proven record.**
2. CJ is a U.S. Army combat veteran and community builder.
3. Vance County has talent and a strong workforce; it needs opportunity.
4. Neighborhood progress should restore pride and return value to residents.
5. Youth work is built on literacy, mentoring, safety, and career pathways.
6. Safe communities grow from relationships, opportunity, and changed mindsets.

## Campaign status

Use exactly: **On the ballot November 3, 2026.**

Do not use petition language, petition deadlines, or calls to sign a petition.

## Calls to action

- Primary: **Meet CJ**
- Secondary: **Volunteer**
- Persistent utility action: **Donate**

## Content guardrails

- Never invent military dates, ranks, units, awards, diagnoses, treatment, or
  recovery details.
- Never imply that a partner organization endorses the campaign.
- Treat client-provided metrics as approval-gated claims and keep their status in
  `docs/content-claims.md`.
- Do not publish bank details, private donor data, volunteer submissions, or
  service-account information.
- The original, owner-supplied campaign photograph is required before launch.
